The Most Dumbest Trading Card Game

Hey, welcome. You just tore open a pack of cards and now you're holding this, wondering what you got yourself into. Fair question. The short answer: betting, chaos, and a card called Greg that may ruin your night. Read this once, you'll be fine. Probably.
🃏 Two Kinds of Cards
⚔️ Fighters
The cards that actually throw hands. Each one has an ATK number (bigger = angrier) and an ability.
⚡ Twists
Pure chaos in card form. Zero restrictions on timing — play one literally whenever you feel like it, rules be darned.

🎮 How A Round Works
1
Draw some cards
Your very first turn, draw 7 cards to make your hand. Every round after that, draw back up to 7 (if you already have 7 or more, skip drawing). You can hold up to 10 cards max at any time.
2
Pick a Fighter card and hide it
Look through your hand, choose one Fighter card, and set it face down in front of you like it's a secret you're taking to the grave. Nobody sees it yet — not your friends, not your enemies, not your mom. Once the table agrees to move on to betting, that's your Fighter. No swapping it out, no "wait, actually" — you made your bed.
💡 How to not pick badly:
Bigger ATK numbers usually win fights, shockingly. But a sneaky little card with the right ability can absolutely clown a bigger number. Read the room, trust your gut, and pick something you won't regret flipping over.
3
Everybody places a bet
Put down at least 1 betcoin on whoever you think is going to win this round — yourself, your best friend, your worst enemy, doesn't matter. You can't bet 0 (coward move anyway), and you can't bet more than you've got. Once the table agrees to move on to the flip, your bet is locked. No refunds, no do-overs, no tears.
4
Everyone flips their card together (also called the Reveal Phase)
Count down out loud like you're launching a rocket — "Three, Two, One, Flip!" — and turn your card face up at the exact same moment as everyone else. Flip early and the whole table will know you're a cheater. Word travels fast.
5
Abilities go off
Every Fighter's ability (also called its Trait) fires automatically, whether you like it or not. You don't get a veto. These all happen at basically the same time, like a very confusing fireworks show. If two abilities are arguing over the same thing and genuinely can't both win, flip a real coin and let fate decide.
6
Highest number wins
After all the powers have had their moment, whoever's left with the highest ATK wins the round and gets to feel smug about it. They collect every bet that was placed, plus 2 free bonus betcoins just for being right. Tie? Everyone who tied gets their bet back plus 1 free coin each — a polite, awkward group hug of a result.
7
Clean up and go again
The Fighter card you played goes face-up onto your own personal discard pile — out in the open, no hiding it now. Check if someone reached 25 (brag rights) or dropped to 0 (yikes), then do the whole thing over again.

⚡ About Twist Cards
Twist cards are the chaos button, and nobody's stopping you from mashing it. Here's the deal:
⏰
There is NO timing rule. None. Zero.
Play a Twist the second you draw your hand. Play one mid-bet. Play one while someone's mid-sentence explaining their strategy. Play one after the cards have already flipped face up — yes, really, after the reveal, after you've seen the result, it's still legal. If the round hasn't fully ended, you can slam down a Twist. There is no "right moment." Every moment is the right moment.
🧩
They absolutely stack
Multiple Twists, multiple players, same round — go nuts. They all take effect at once, piling on top of each other like a confused game of Jenga. If two Twists genuinely can't both happen, flip a coin and let the universe sort it out.
⏳
They wear off (eventually)
A Twist's effect only lasts for that one round, unless the card specifically brags about lasting longer.
🗑️
Once played, it's gone forever
A played Twist goes straight to the discard pile. No take-backs, no "wait I didn't mean it," no mercy.
💀 Hit 0 Betcoins? Not Dead Yet.
This is called The Really Epic Last Stand — your dramatic, last-ditch, everything-on-the-line moment of glory (or humiliation).
1
Pick one other player at the table to duel. Pick your rival. Pick your best friend. Pick whoever looks the most nervous.
2
Only the two of you flip cards. Nobody bets. Everyone else just watches in silence, eating snacks, judging you. You can still play Twists, because of course you can.
✓
Win and you stroll back into the game with 5 betcoins like nothing happened.
✗
Lose and you're done. Permanently. Go get a snack, you've earned the rest.
🔎
Discard piles are public. Snooping through them isn't cheating, it's basic survival instinct.
